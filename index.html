Logistics payments platform

Catch nearly half of all chargebacks by reviewing 5% of payments

The problem
Fewer than 1 in 300 payments charge back, but under the platform's chargeback guarantee each one is a direct loss. Blunt rules annoyed good customers and missed the real problem transactions.
The work
Built a scoring model that judges each payment on its own merits at the moment of payment, works on brand-new accounts, and explains every flag.
The result
The riskiest 5% of payments get a light check, such as a zip code or SMS confirm. Payments are never blocked, and the other 95% see no friction.
50%of all chargebacks caught inside the riskiest 5% of payments
10×better than reviewing transactions at random
40×more likely to dispute again once a phone number has charged back
